# ExamPilot — User Guide
**GT InnoX LLP — Understand Questions. Learn the Answers.**

Welcome to ExamPilot, your offline-first AI-powered learning and MCQ practice assistant.

---

## 1. Core Workflow: Capturing & Analyzing Questions

ExamPilot provides three flexible ways to analyze multiple choice practice questions:

### Method A: Screen Capture (Global Hotkey)
1. Whenever a practice question is on your screen, press:
   ```
   Ctrl + Shift + Q
   ```
2. The screen dims with an interactive selection overlay.
3. Drag a box enclosing the question and all its options.
4. Release the mouse. The image is captured and processed through local OCR.
5. Review the extracted question text and options in the **Practice Assistant**.
6. Click **Analyse Question with Local AI** to receive:
   - Suggested Answer with option letter and text.
   - Calibrated Confidence indicator.
   - Pedagogical explanation explaining why the answer is correct.
   - Option-by-option breakdown explaining why each remaining option is flawed.

### Method B: Upload an Image
1. In the **Practice Assistant**, click **Upload Image File**.
2. Select any PNG, JPG, or screenshot.
3. The OCR engine extracts and parses the text into structured options automatically.

### Method C: Manual Entry or Paste
1. Type or paste your question text into the question box.
2. Enter or edit the options in boxes A, B, C, D (or click `+ Add Option` for option E).
3. Select your subject category from the dropdown.
4. Click **Analyse Question with Local AI**.

---

## 2. Floating Learning Assistant Panel

For non-intrusive practice while reading educational materials:
- Click **Floating Mode** in the top-right header or sidebar.
- A compact, draggable widget appears.
- Pin it on top using the **Pin** checkbox.
- Trigger `Ctrl + Shift + Q` directly to analyze questions without switching full windows.

---

## 3. Question History & Export

- Navigate to **Question History** in the sidebar.
- Filter questions by subject or search by keyword in question text or explanations.
- Double-click any row to view full option breakdowns and reasoning.
- Click **Export (CSV/JSON)** to save your practice history for offline study or spaced repetition.
- Click **Clear All History** to wipe all stored questions immediately.

---

## 4. Reporting Incorrect Answers

ExamPilot is designed for pedagogical rigor. If an AI explanation appears inaccurate:
1. In the Practice Assistant, click **Report / Flag Incorrect Answer**.
2. Select what you believe is the correct option and provide notes.
3. The question will be marked as `Needs Review`, and practice accuracy metrics will adjust accordingly.

---

## 5. Permitted Practice Environment Policy

> **Important**: ExamPilot is strictly intended for practice environments and formative assessments where AI assistance is explicitly authorized. The application **does not**:
> - Circumvent proctoring or supervision software.
> - Hide its operation or conceal processes.
> - Automatically click or submit examination forms.
> - Intercept keystrokes or inspect unrelated browser tabs.
