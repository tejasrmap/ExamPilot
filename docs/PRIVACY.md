# ExamPilot — Privacy & Data Protection Policy
**GT InnoX LLP — Understand Questions. Learn the Answers.**

*Effective Date: November 2026*  
*Product: ExamPilot (Windows Desktop Application & Chrome Extension)*

---

## 1. Core Commitment: Offline-First Architecture

ExamPilot is designed and distributed by **GT InnoX LLP** as an offline-first learning tool. We believe students and professionals should be able to learn and practice with state-of-the-art AI without surrendering their data, study habits, or intellectual property to cloud services.

---

## 2. Data We DO NOT Collect

ExamPilot does NOT:
- Transmit question text, answers, or study history to cloud servers.
- Use OpenAI, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any paid external AI API.
- Collect passwords, browser cookies, browsing history, or payment credentials.
- Silently monitor browser tabs or run background screen surveillance.
- Evade examination restrictions or circumvent proctoring software.
- Include analytics trackers, telemetric beacons, or third-party advertising SDKs.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your local computer at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ExamPilot\`:

1. **Practice History (`exampilot.db`)**:
   - Multiple-choice questions captured or entered by the user.
   - Suggested answers, explanations, and confidence scores generated by your local model.
   - User-submitted flags, corrections, or notes.
2. **Application Settings**:
   - Preferred keyboard shortcut (`Ctrl+Shift+Q`).
   - Selected local Ollama model name.
   - Local database backup files if explicitly exported by you.
3. **Application Logs (`logs/exampilot.log`)**:
   - Technical diagnostic records with automatic redaction of emails, tokens, and sensitive strings.
   - Logs are rotated locally (max 5 MB) and never transmitted off-device.

---

## 4. Chrome Extension Permissions Justification

The ExamPilot Chrome Extension requests the minimum permissions required to function:
- `nativeMessaging`: Enables communication between the browser popup and your installed desktop ExamPilot engine via standard Windows pipes.
- `activeTab`: Used strictly when you click "Extract Selected Question" to read text you have actively highlighted on a permitted practice page.
- `storage`: Retains UI preferences (e.g. active tab selection) within your local browser profile.

The extension never inspects tabs in the background without explicit user clicks.

---

## 5. User Control & Instant Data Erasure

You maintain complete ownership and control of your practice history:
- **Instant History Wipe**: Click **Clear All History** in the Settings or History tab to permanently delete all stored questions, sessions, and feedback via a secure SQLite purge.
- **Full Database Export**: You can export your data at any time in standard CSV or JSON format.
- **Uninstallation**: Running `scripts/uninstall.bat` prompts you whether to delete or keep your local database.
